04. At return

When packs come back, the history doesn't.

A warranty dispute

Two questions, no single record to answer them

What happened to this pack in the field? What happened to it on the floor when it was built?

Investigations stretch on, and the call between misuse and a genuine defect is hard to defend.

And a recall

Scoped on assumptions, not traceable data

Without a link from each pack to its build history, the only safe recall is a wide one.

Good packs come back with the bad, and the cost lands on the manufacturer.
