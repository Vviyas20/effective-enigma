03. In the field

Packs ship out. Usage data rarely comes back.

Once packs are in the market, telematics stays with the OEMs. The manufacturer who built the pack seldom sees how it is actually used.

What R&D has today

Lab simulations and bench tests

Controlled cycles, fixed temperatures, assumed duty profiles. Useful, but narrow.

What R&D is missing

Real-world usage, at scale

How packs age across usage patterns, climates, regions and demographics. The input the next cell or pack design needs.
