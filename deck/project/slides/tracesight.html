TraceSight captures a digital identity for each battery with the static and dynamic data it needs. Warranty disputes can be separated into misuse versus authentic manufacturing defects. The same identity serves the EU Battery Passport for exports to Europe, the upcoming Battery Aadhaar, and makes EPR compliance easier to calculate and report to the CPCB portal.
