Fast lines, scattered data.

To keep up with demand, lines run at pace. The data they produce lands wherever it can: MES, SCADA, tester logs, and in some cases a spreadsheet.

A batch fails self-discharge testing at the end of the line.

Which station, cell lot or shift caused it?
